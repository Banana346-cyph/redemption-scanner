# RUT: Redemption Utility Tool

Phone app for the Redemption card game: scan a card to identify it, see its Your Turn Games price, build and share trades, and track your collection.

- Card names, types, prices and pictures come from Your Turn Games' store listing, refreshed every night by a GitHub Action.
- Everything runs on the phone; the text reader is Tesseract.js. Your collection is saved on your phone (use Backup in the Collection tab).
- Install: open the site on your phone and follow the steps it shows (Add to Home Screen on iPhone, Install app on Android).
