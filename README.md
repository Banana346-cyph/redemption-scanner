# Redemption Scanner

Phone web app for the Redemption card game: scan a card to identify it, see its Your Turn Games price, and build trades.

- Card names and prices come from Your Turn Games' store listings (snapshot in `cards.json`).
- Everything runs on the phone; the text reader is Tesseract.js.
- Install: open the site in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose **Add to Home Screen** / **Install app**.
